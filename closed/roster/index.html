The Path

The Closed Ladder

This is how your time on the roster runs. Day ranges are conservative, most people move quicker. It runs as a 2-month cohort: the first stretch gets you signed and started, the second ramps you up on your offer so you actually perform.

01
Days 1 to 10

Prospect

You join the roster. Sales training, the Identity Shift module, and we build your resume and Loom with you. Goal: application ready in 14 days or less.

02
Around days 14 to 30

Signed

We put you in front of vetted offers and prep you for every interview. Goal: you're signed to a real offer.

03
Days 30 to 60

Starter

We learn your offer with you and train you on it: the script, the objections, the buyer. You send us your calls, we review them. Goal: you're performing, not just employed.

04
Around day 60

Top Rep

You're one of the best reps on your offer. That's the target for everyone on the roster.

05
After 60 days

Transfer

Once you're putting up numbers, you have the opportunity to have us move you up. Setter to closer, closer to better closer, smaller offer to bigger offer.

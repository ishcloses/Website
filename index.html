Meet the founders

Questions.

Short, honest answers. Anything else, ask us on the call.

What's the goal of this?

We build you a predictable client acquisition channel. You focus on taking the sales calls and scaling.

Why Meta and not LinkedIn?

Your buyers are on Instagram and Facebook every day, not only when they're at their desk. Reaching them there usually costs a lot less than LinkedIn, and once the system learns what a qualified booking looks like, Meta gets very good at finding more people like them.

What do I actually own?

Everything we build: the ad account, the pixel and its data, the landing page and quiz, the CRM and its automations, and every creative. It's set up in your name from day one, so if we ever part ways, it all stays with you.

How fast can we launch?

Within the first 3 weeks. The offer and the funnel come first, so the ads have something worth sending people to.

What if I don't have an offer yet?

That's the part most people get stuck on, and it's where this helps most. You answer questions about who you help and what you sell, and scalD builds the promise, the guarantee and the pricing around it.

Ready to stopchasing clients?

One call. We'll look at your offer and your numbers, and show you what your channel would look like.

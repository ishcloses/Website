Ismail Ali

Co-founder and CEO

"Content, ads and funnels don't scale a business on their own."

I started in sales, not marketing.

As a teenager I filed four patents and pitched VCs. At 17 I was knocking doors in the Texas heat selling insurance. I was the youngest and the top rep on several sales teams, and I went on to build my own sales agency.

As the top sales closer, manager and offer owner, I learned sales processes inside and out, and I know exactly what a qualified lead looks like for every industry. That's what I make sure scalD delivers to every client.

That's where I learned the thing most agencies miss. Content, ads and funnels don't scale a business on their own. They just bring more people to a broken sales process.

Nizal Dawood

Co-founder and CEO

"Most people don't need a better product. They need a way to sell it."

I'm Nizal Dawood, co-founder of scalD. I started in door-to-door sales and did six figures in my first year with my own D2D company, managing a sales team of seven. That's when I realized sales is my forte.

After that I ran luxury car rentals, learned high-ticket sales, and worked as a sales executive for AI companies.

Through all of it, I saw that most people don't need a better product. They need a way to sell it. I wanted to help people through the power of scaling, so I started scalD with my partner Ismail.
